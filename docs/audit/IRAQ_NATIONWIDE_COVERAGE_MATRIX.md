# Nationwide Iraq catalogue coverage matrix

Reviewed 2026-10-09. **Verified count means records individually represented
in the candidate files, not a claim of complete coverage.** All 19
governorate names have government-source evidence: the 18 names in an older
[COSIT statistical publication](https://cosit.gov.iq/documents/indices/CPI/%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%B1%20%D9%81%D9%8A%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%82/%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%B1%20%D9%81%D9%8A%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%82%20%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9%202018.pdf)
and [Official Gazette issue 4825](https://moj.gov.iq/view.9248/) for
Halabja as the nineteenth governorate. The old statistical publication does
**not** verify current district/subdistrict boundaries or counts.

Institution counts below are source-attributed **candidate sightings** from
page 1 of the Ministry [public](https://mohesr.gov.iq/ar/home/public_universities)
and [private](https://mohesr.gov.iq/ar/home/private_universities/) lists.
They are neither complete per-governorate counts nor approved imports.
All administrative subdivisions, residential neighborhoods and pickup areas
remain unverified as import-ready rows.

The [open-admin-data dataset](https://github.com/open-admin-data/iraq-administrative-divisions)
publishes **18** governorates, 101 districts and 294 subdistricts, last
updated 2026-09-08, under CC BY 4.0. Those are **community dataset totals**,
not current government-verified DarbGo counts; Halabja is absent. The
[Iraqi Open Source dataset](https://github.com/Iraqi-Open-Source/official-iraqi-governorates)
claims 19 governorates with MIT licensing, but its subdivisions, proposed
Halabja code and provenance need government cross-checks. Neither supplies a
verified nationwide residential-neighborhood catalogue.
