A few things you might be wondering.

A clear path, from the start.From your first search to your first request.
What is DarbGo?

DarbGo is an Iraq-focused university transportation platform that connects students with reviewed route drivers travelling from their area to their university.

How do I join a route?

Choose your area and university, compare matching routes, then send a join request. Your seat is only confirmed when the driver accepts your request.

Where can I see schedules and prices?

Each route presents its schedule, price, and available seats so you can compare your options before requesting to join.

Can I register as a driver?

Yes. Register your details, add your vehicle and route, and submit your application for administrator approval before receiving student requests.

Are drivers approved before joining?

Driver applications are reviewed by administrators before drivers are approved to offer routes. Always review your route details and confirm arrangements before traveling.

Can I book a real ride on this page?

This is an interactive product preview. Search results, counters, and testimonials are illustrative. No real booking, payment, or driver application is submitted here.
